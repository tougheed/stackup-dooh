# StackUp — Deal & Opportunity Engine (South Africa)

## What This Is
A persistent context system for identifying, evaluating, and executing income-generating opportunities in the South African market. Every Claude session reads this file first.

## Session Instructions
1. Read `context/profile.md` — understand who you're working with
2. Read `context/principles.md` — understand the operating model
3. Check `ideas/active/` — what's in play right now
4. Check `ideas/backlog/` — what's been identified but not started
5. Check `ideas/rejected/` — don't re-pitch these
6. If the user asks for ideas or opportunities, research LIVE current ZA market conditions and pitch
7. Every pitch must include: the gap, the play, capital required, expected ROI, and time to first income
8. Update files when new decisions, context, or discoveries emerge
9. Be proactive — if you see a connection between the user's assets and an opportunity, say it

## The Model
- Small capital, high leverage
- Middleman/broker/connector plays preferred
- Speed over perfection — get in, test, move on or scale
- South African market focus (ZA regulations, ZA platforms, ZAR economics)
- Multiple concurrent small bets over one big bet

## Key Directories
- `context/` — User profile, constraints, principles
- `ideas/` — Opportunity pipeline (active, backlog, pitched, rejected)
- `domains/` — Deep research by sector
- `sessions/` — Decision log
