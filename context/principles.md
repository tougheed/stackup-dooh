# Operating Principles

## Deal Evaluation Criteria
Every opportunity must answer:
1. **What's the gap?** — Who needs what, and who has it, and why aren't they connected?
2. **What's the play?** — How do we sit in the middle and add value?
3. **Capital required?** — In ZAR, what's the minimum to test this?
4. **Time to first income?** — Days, weeks, or months?
5. **Scalability?** — Can this grow, or is it a one-shot?
6. **Risk?** — What kills this deal?
7. **Moat?** — What stops someone else from doing it tomorrow?

## Preferred Deal Structures
- Broker/middleman — connect buyer to seller, take commission
- Arbitrage — buy low in one market, sell higher in another
- Service layer — add a thin service on top of existing supply (logistics, vetting, aggregation)
- Digital enablement — put an offline process online and charge for access
- Tender subcontracting — win or access tenders, subcontract the work

## Kill Criteria (don't pursue if)
- Requires more than R50,000 upfront (adjustable — update this number)
- Takes more than 3 months to first revenue
- Requires a team of more than 2 to operate
- Regulatory barrier is high with no clear path
- Market is already saturated with well-funded competitors
