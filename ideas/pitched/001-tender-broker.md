# Idea 001: Government Tender Broker / Subcontract Connector

## Status: PITCHED

## The Gap
R1.07 trillion in infrastructure spend over 3 years (2026 budget). Contracts over R30M MUST subcontract 30% to EMEs/QSEs. Big contractors need compliant subcontractors. Small businesses don't know how to find or respond to tenders. Neither side is efficiently connected.

## The Play
Build a matchmaking service between main contractors who need to meet their 30% subcontracting obligation and registered EMEs/QSEs who can do the work. You're the broker — you vet the small guys, package them for the big guys, and take a finder's fee or percentage.

## How It Works
1. Register on etenders.gov.za, ProTenders, EasyTenders — monitor new tenders daily
2. Identify contracts with mandatory subcontracting requirements
3. Build a database of verified EMEs/QSEs (CIPC registered, tax compliant, B-BBEE rated)
4. Approach main contractors with a curated shortlist for their subcontracting needs
5. Charge the subcontractor a placement fee (5-10% of contract value) or the main contractor a sourcing fee

## Capital Required
- R5,000–R10,000 (website, CIPC registration, initial marketing)
- Your VPS can host the matching platform

## Time to First Income
4-8 weeks (time to build database and land first placement)

## Revenue Model
- 5-10% placement fee on subcontract value
- Average subcontract on a R30M tender = R9M, so 30% sub = R2.7M minimum
- Even 5% of a R500K subcontract = R25,000 per placement

## Risk
- Long payment cycles in government work (30-90 days)
- Need to vet subcontractors properly or your reputation dies
- Competition from established BEE consultancies

## Moat
- Relationship depth with both sides
- Quality of your vetted database
- Speed — first to connect wins

3. Energy — R213.6 billion
