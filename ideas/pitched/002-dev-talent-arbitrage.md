# Idea 002: SA Developer Talent Arbitrage

## Status: PITCHED

## The Gap
SA developers charge median R350/hr (~$18.60). Global clients on Upwork pay $50-$100/hr for equivalent work. SA has English fluency, timezone overlap with EU, and 73% of freelancers have 5+ years experience. But many SA devs don't know how to market themselves internationally, and foreign clients don't know how to find vetted SA talent.

## The Play
Recruit SA developers, package them professionally (portfolio, CV, code samples), and place them with US/EU clients at international rates. You take the spread.

## How It Works
1. Recruit devs from OfferZen, LinkedIn SA, local dev communities — target those earning R350-500/hr locally
2. Vet them (code test, interview, reference check)
3. Create professional profiles positioned for international clients
4. List on Upwork, Toptal, or direct outreach to startups/agencies needing dev capacity
5. Bill client at $45-65/hr, pay dev R500-700/hr (~$26-37), pocket the difference

## Capital Required
- R5,000–R15,000 (platform fees, initial marketing, legal — basic contractor agreements)

## Time to First Income
2-4 weeks (one placement = income)

## Revenue Model
- Spread of $15-30/hr per developer placed
- One dev working full-time (160hrs/month) = $2,400-$4,800/month margin
- Scale to 5 devs = $12,000-$24,000/month
- In ZAR at current rates: R220K-R440K/month at 5 devs

## Risk
- Client finds dev directly, cuts you out (mitigate with contracts)
- Dev quality inconsistency
- Payment collection from international clients
- Competition from Andela, Trio, Arc.dev

## Moat
- Personal vetting and relationship with devs
- Niche specialisation (e.g., only AI/ML devs, or only mobile devs)
- Faster placement than big agencies
