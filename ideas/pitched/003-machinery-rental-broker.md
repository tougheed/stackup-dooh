# Idea 003: Construction Machinery Rental Broker

## Status: PITCHED

## The Gap
SA construction equipment rental market = R8.5B (USD 462M) in 2026, growing 7.18% CAGR. Small contractors in rural/peri-urban areas can't access rental equipment — big rental companies (Bell, Barloworld) focus on major metros and large contracts. Meanwhile, equipment owners (farmers, retired contractors) have idle machines sitting in yards.

## The Play
Peer-to-peer machinery rental marketplace. Connect equipment owners with contractors who need short-term rentals. You broker the deal, handle the paperwork, take a commission.

## How It Works
1. Canvas equipment owners in your region — farmers with TLBs, graders, excavators sitting idle
2. List their equipment on a simple platform (even WhatsApp Business initially)
3. Market to small contractors doing residential, small commercial, and municipal work
4. Handle booking, basic insurance/liability agreement, and payment
5. Take 15-20% commission on each rental

## Capital Required
- R5,000–R20,000 (website/app, marketing, basic legal for rental agreements)
- Could start with just a WhatsApp Business number and a spreadsheet

## Time to First Income
2-4 weeks (one rental deal = income)

## Revenue Model
- TLB rental: R3,500-R5,500/day. Your 15% = R525-R825/day
- Excavator: R5,000-R12,000/day. Your 15% = R750-R1,800/day
- 10 rental days per month = R5,250-R18,000/month
- Scale with more equipment owners = linear growth

## Risk
- Equipment damage liability — need solid rental agreements
- Owner doesn't maintain equipment, contractor gets burned
- Insurance complexity
- Seasonal (construction slows in heavy rain)

## Moat
- Local relationships on both sides
- Trust — you're the known middleman
- Expand to include operators (many owners will supply with operator)

## Why Now
R1.07 trillion infrastructure spend = surge in small contractor demand for equipment they can't afford to buy.
