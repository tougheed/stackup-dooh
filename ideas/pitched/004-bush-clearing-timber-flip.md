# Idea 004: Bush Clearing + Timber/Biomass Flip

## Status: PITCHED

## The Gap
Land developers, farmers, and municipalities pay R850-R15,000+ per job for bush/tree clearing. The cleared wood is usually left as waste or burned. But that wood has value — mulch, biomass fuel, firewood, charcoal — if someone collects and sells it.

## The Play
Offer bush clearing services at competitive rates (or even free for large jobs) in exchange for keeping the cleared timber. Then process and sell the wood as mulch, firewood, or biomass chips.

## How It Works
1. Market bush clearing services to developers, farmers, municipalities, Eskom (servitude clearing)
2. Price competitively — your real margin is in the timber, not the clearing fee
3. For large jobs, offer FREE clearing in exchange for timber rights
4. Process timber: chip for mulch/biomass, cut for firewood, or kiln for charcoal
5. Sell to: nurseries (mulch), braai wood retailers, biomass energy buyers, charcoal exporters

## Capital Required
- R15,000–R50,000 (chainsaw, transport, basic chipping if scaling)
- Can start with just a chainsaw and bakkie, subcontract the heavy clearing

## Time to First Income
1-2 weeks (clearing jobs are immediate, firewood sells same day)

## Revenue Model
- Clearing fee: R2,000-R8,000 per job (or R0 if taking timber)
- Firewood: R80-R150 per bag, one large tree = 20-50 bags = R1,600-R7,500
- Mulch: R300-R600 per cubic meter
- Biomass chips: sold by ton to industrial buyers
- Combined: R10,000-R30,000/month starting small

## Risk
- Physical/dangerous work — need proper safety gear and insurance
- Protected tree species — must check municipal lists before clearing
- Permits required for commercial timber in some areas (DAFF)
- Seasonal demand fluctuation

## Moat
- Relationships with developers who need regular clearing
- Locked-in supply agreements with mulch/firewood buyers
- Equipment ownership over time

## Government Support
- DAFF Forestry Enterprise Development (FED) programme provides support
- SEDA and IDC available for financial assistance
