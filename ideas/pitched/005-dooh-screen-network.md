# Idea 005: Micro DOOH Screen Network

## Status: PITCHED

## The Gap
SA DOOH market = R4.6B (USD 258M) in 2026. Programmatic digital screens now allow 48-hour ad bursts that used to need six-figure budgets. But most DOOH inventory is owned by big players (Primedia, JCDecaux). Small venue owners (spazas, salons, gyms, waiting rooms, taxi ranks) have foot traffic but no screens. Local businesses want to advertise but can't afford traditional DOOH rates.

## The Play
Place cheap Android screens in high-traffic small venues. Sell ad space to local businesses. You own the network, venues get a revenue share, advertisers get hyper-local reach.

## How It Works
1. Buy cheap Android tablets/screens (R1,500-R3,000 each)
2. Install free digital signage software (e.g., Yodeck, ScreenCloud — free tiers exist)
3. Place in venues with foot traffic: salons, spazas, medical waiting rooms, taxi ranks
4. Venue gets 20-30% of ad revenue or free content (news, entertainment)
5. Sell 15-30 second ad slots to local businesses (restaurants, mechanics, lawyers, etc.)
6. Manage content remotely from your VPS

## Capital Required
- R10,000–R30,000 for 5-10 screens + installation
- Your VPS runs the content management

## Time to First Income
2-4 weeks (sell ads before installing — pre-sell to fund hardware)

## Revenue Model
- Per screen: 10 ad slots × R500-R1,500/month per slot = R5,000-R15,000/month per screen
- Venue share (25%): R1,250-R3,750
- Your gross per screen: R3,750-R11,250/month
- 10 screens: R37,500-R112,500/month gross
- Hardware payback: 1-2 months per screen

## Risk
- Theft/damage of screens in some locations
- Selling ads is the hard part — need to hustle local businesses
- Venues lose interest if they don't see revenue
- Power/connectivity issues in some locations

## Moat
- Venue relationships and exclusivity agreements
- Network density in a specific area (more screens = more value to advertisers)
- Local knowledge — you know which corners have traffic

## Why This Fits You
- This is literally what stackup-dooh was named for
- Your VPS can serve as the content management backend
- Low per-unit cost, scalable, recurring revenue
