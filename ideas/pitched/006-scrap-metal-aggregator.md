# Idea 006: Registered Scrap Metal Aggregator

## Status: PITCHED

## The Gap
New 2026 regulations tightened scrap metal trading — both buyers AND sellers must now register, cash transactions are banned, and monthly electronic reporting is mandatory via the Metal Trading System. This killed many informal operators overnight. Compliant, registered dealers are now in short supply, especially ones who can aggregate from small collectors.

## The Play
Get fully registered and compliant (Second-Hand Goods Act + SAPS license + tax compliance). Become the middleman between small collectors/waste pickers and large recyclers/smelters. Your compliance IS your product.

## How It Works
1. Register with CIPC, get Second-Hand Goods dealer license from SAPS, ensure tax compliance
2. Set up a collection point or do mobile collection rounds
3. Buy from registered small collectors (or help them register — value-add service)
4. Aggregate volumes and sell to large registered recyclers at better per-kg rates
5. Handle the mandatory Metal Trading System reporting

## Capital Required
- R10,000–R30,000 (registration fees, scale, transport, initial buying capital)
- Ongoing buying capital recycles as you sell

## Time to First Income
4-8 weeks (registration process + first collection round)

## Revenue Model
- Copper: buy at R80-100/kg from collectors, sell at R120-140/kg to recyclers
- Aluminium: buy at R15-20/kg, sell at R25-35/kg
- Steel: lower margin but high volume
- R20-40/kg spread on copper × 500kg/month = R10,000-R20,000/month starting
- Scale with volume and more collection points

## Risk
- Registration process can be slow (SAPS, CIPC)
- Stolen goods risk — must verify sources meticulously (legal liability)
- Price volatility tied to global commodity markets
- Physical infrastructure needed (scale, storage)
- Heavy regulation — non-compliance = shutdown

## Moat
- Compliance barrier is now HIGH — most small operators can't or won't register
- Relationship with verified small collectors
- Reporting capability (most small players can't do the Metal Trading System reporting)

## Regulatory Reality Check
- MUST only trade with other registered dealers
- NO cash transactions
- Monthly electronic reporting to ITAC
- Copper has extra scrutiny due to cable theft crisis
