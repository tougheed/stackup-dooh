# Idea 007: Marketplace Arbitrage — Underpriced Flips

## Status: PITCHED

## The Core Insight
There are 5 proven channels in SA where assets sell consistently below market value. The play is buying from distressed/motivated sellers and reselling through the right channel at fair market price.

---

## Channel 1: Solar/Inverter Dump (BEST TIMING RIGHT NOW)

### Why it's underpriced
- 1.5 million solar installations happened during loadshedding crisis (2022-2024)
- Loadshedding has reduced — people who panic-bought are now offloading
- Inverter prices dropped 20-35% since 2022 from competition
- Solar e-waste is becoming a problem — people want this stuff GONE
- "Urgent sale" listings on Gumtree: 5kW inverters + batteries for R10K-R20K that cost R35K-R57K new

### The play
- Monitor Gumtree/FB Marketplace for urgent solar/inverter listings daily
- Buy barely-used systems (many have <6 months use) at panic prices
- Resell to: (a) businesses still installing solar, (b) rural/peri-urban buyers who couldn't afford new, (c) export to rest of Africa where loadshedding is still chronic
- Markup: 40-80% on purchase price

### Capital: R10K-R30K buying stock
### Time to flip: Same week

---

## Channel 2: Bank Repo & Deceased Estate Auctions

### Why it's underpriced
- Banks want cash recovery fast, not best price
- Reserve prices are 10-20% below retail trade value
- Deceased estates must liquidate — executors want it done
- Most buyers are dealers — individual buyers face less competition on equipment/tools

### The play
- Register with SA Auction Group (R10K refundable deposit), Auction Operation, Park Village Auctions
- Focus on: vehicles (bakkies especially), construction equipment, tools, commercial kitchen equipment
- Buy at auction, list on Gumtree/FB Marketplace/AutoTrader at retail
- Bakkies: buy repo at R80K-R120K, sell retail at R130K-R180K

### Platforms
- saauctiongroup.co.za
- auctionoperation.co.za
- parkvillageauctions.co.za
- repossessed.co.za
- wesbank.co.za/auctions
- gobid.co.za

### Capital: R10K deposit + R30K-R100K first purchase
### Time to flip: 1-4 weeks

---

## Channel 3: Customer Return Pallets

### Why it's underpriced
- Retailers can't resell returns as new (CPA 30-day returns policy)
- Items are working but opened/used — sold in bulk at 70-90% discount
- Electronics, appliances, tools — all have strong secondhand demand

### The play
- Buy from ReturnsToBuy (returnstobuy.co.za) or Lower (lower.co.za)
- Cherry-pick working items from mixed pallets
- Test, clean, photograph, list individually on Gumtree/FB Marketplace
- Typical: buy pallet for R2K-R5K, individual items resell for R8K-R15K total

### Capital: R2K-R10K per pallet
### Time to flip: 1-2 weeks

---

## Channel 4: FB Marketplace / Gumtree "Must Go" Listings

### Why it's underpriced
- People emigrating (huge wave of SA emigration = fire sales)
- Divorces, evictions, business closures — motivated sellers
- Sellers who just want it gone TODAY, will take 30-50% of value

### What to hunt
- Search terms: "urgent sale", "must go", "make an offer", "emigrating", "moving abroad"
- Best categories: power tools (Makita, Bosch, DeWalt), generators, commercial kitchen equipment, gym equipment, office furniture
- Power tools: Makita 18V combo sets sell for R2K-R4K "urgent" vs R6K-R8K retail

### The play
- Set up saved searches / alerts on Gumtree and FB Marketplace
- Respond FAST — first to call gets the deal
- Negotiate hard on "must go" listings — offer cash, same-day pickup
- Resell on same platforms at fair market price (or hold for seasonal demand)

### Capital: R5K-R20K rolling stock
### Time to flip: Days

---

## Channel 5: Government Surplus Auctions

### Why it's underpriced
- Government fleet vehicles and equipment disposed of by regulation, not market need
- Often well-maintained but high-mileage — priced on KM, not condition
- Equipment (office furniture, IT gear, tools) goes for cents on the rand

### The play
- Monitor etenders.gov.za for disposal notices
- Attend SA Auction Group government vehicle auctions
- Focus on: Toyota Hilux/Quantum (always in demand), office furniture (resell to SMMEs), IT equipment (refurb and resell)

### Capital: R10K deposit + purchase funds
### Time to flip: 1-4 weeks

---

## How to Systematise This

You can automate the sourcing on your VPS:
1. Scrape Gumtree/FB Marketplace for trigger keywords ("urgent", "must go", "emigrating")
2. Alert yourself via Telegram/WhatsApp when underpriced listings appear
3. Track buy/sell prices in a simple spreadsheet to know your margins
4. Reinvest profits into higher-value items as capital grows

This is a CASH FLOW business — capital recycles fast, margins are known, risk is low if you inspect before buying.
